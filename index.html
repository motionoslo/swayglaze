about sway glaze

born from
a love of hair.

for sophie, hair has always been more than a routine. it is confidence, self-expression and the feeling of walking into a room as your best self. she wanted it all in one routine: real care, visible shine, textures that feel like a treat and a scent worth noticing. sway glaze is that routine.

inspired by the way she cares for her skin, sophie set out to give hair the same attention. every sway glaze formula is built on vitamins, amino acids, proteins and nourishing oils, so your hair doesn't just look beautiful, it feels cared for from root to tip.

scent was just as essential. sophie wanted a fragrance that leaves an impression, the kind that makes someone pause as you pass and ask what's in your hair. after nearly two years of creating, testing and perfecting, sway glaze is here, made for everyone who wants to love their hair a little more every day.

“care you can feel, shine you can see, a scent you won’t forget.”
sophie stray
